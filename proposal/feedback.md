## Feedback

This is a nice, focused extension project with a very clean experimental plan. For the experiments, the two details that matter most are how you compute IATs at the flow level (for example, whether you treat bidirectional traffic separately and how you handle retransmissions or zero-length gaps), since inconsistent IAT extraction will ripple directly into the feature quality, and how you align the SYN-packet subset with the streaming-service labels, because errors in mapping those early packets to the correct session will dominate any observed accuracy difference between the baseline and the extended feature set. One more important thing... in normal cases, beyond the handshake, you only see multiple SYNs when: The client retries because the SYN or SYN-ACK was not received (packet loss) or spurious retransmissions or some sort of network disruptance. So you may not always observe 10 syn in a session flow, so you'll have to find out how to deal with that, either through taking as many syncs as you can or just take the IAT of the first 10 packets, regardless of whether they are syn packets or not. Increasing your IAT calculation window can help a lot too, especially if the session is long.
